- ZXM体系形态支持

#### ✅ 4. 复杂逻辑组合 - 完整实现

```python

# OR逻辑  
condition1 OR condition2 OR condition3

# NOT逻辑
NOT condition1

# 复合逻辑
(condition1 AND condition2) OR (condition3 AND NOT condition4)

# 函数调用
```

```yaml
    value: 100.0
  - logic: AND

    indicator_id: RSI
    period: DAILY
    parameters:
      period: 14
    operator: "<"

  - logic: AND
```
